# Text RPG: Character & Simulation Mode

A comprehensive Python-based text RPG featuring both character-driven gameplay and autonomous world simulation.

## Features

### Core Systems
- **Character Creation**: Create custom characters with unique stats, races, and classes
- **Combat System**: Turn-based combat with special abilities and status effects
- **Inventory Management**: Collect, use, and trade items
- **Quest System**: Dynamic quest generation and tracking
- **Save/Load System**: Persistent game state across sessions

### Advanced Combat
- **Special Abilities**: Unique abilities for each character class
- **Status Effects**: Buffs, debuffs, and environmental effects
- **Critical Hits**: Enhanced damage system with critical strikes
- **Combat Logging**: Detailed combat history and statistics

### Magic System
- **Spell Learning**: Learn spells through scrolls and research
- **Magic Schools**: 8 different schools of magic (Evocation, Abjuration, etc.)
- **Spell Types**: Damage, healing, buff, debuff, and utility spells
- **Mana Management**: Strategic resource management for spellcasting
- **Magic Research**: Invest gold to discover new spells

### Religion System
- **Pantheon of Gods**: 9 deities with different domains and alignments
- **Temple System**: Visit temples to pray, donate, and convert
- **Divine Miracles**: Receive blessings and divine interventions
- **Religious Items**: Holy relics and sacred artifacts
- **Alignment System**: Good, Neutral, and Evil deities

### Reputation System
- **Faction Management**: 9 different factions with unique goals
- **Reputation Levels**: From Hostile to Exalted standing
- **Faction Rewards**: Access to special services and items
- **Reputation History**: Track all reputation changes
- **Faction Quests**: Special quests based on faction standing

### Crafting System
- **Recipe Discovery**: Learn recipes through exploration and experimentation
- **Skill Progression**: Improve crafting skills through practice
- **Material Gathering**: Collect resources from various sources
- **Quality System**: Create items of varying quality
- **Specialization**: Focus on specific crafting disciplines

### Housing System
- **Property Ownership**: Purchase and customize homes
- **Room Management**: Add and upgrade different room types
- **Furniture System**: Decorate with various furniture items
- **Storage Solutions**: Expand inventory through housing
- **Comfort Bonuses**: Rest bonuses and stat improvements

### Companion System
- **Animal Companions**: Recruit and train various animals
- **Companion Types**: Dogs, cats, horses, and exotic creatures
- **Training System**: Improve companion abilities and loyalty
- **Combat Support**: Companions can assist in battle
- **Bonding Mechanics**: Strengthen relationships with companions

### Achievement System
- **Multiple Categories**: Combat, exploration, crafting, social, and collection achievements
- **Progressive Rewards**: Unlock new content through achievements
- **Statistics Tracking**: Monitor progress across all game systems
- **Achievement Points**: Earn points for completing objectives
- **Special Rewards**: Unique items and bonuses for achievements

### Simulation Mode
- **Autonomous World**: NPCs form factions, wage wars, and create stories
- **Dynamic Events**: Random events that affect the world
- **Faction Politics**: Complex relationships between different groups
- **World Evolution**: The world changes over time
- **Statistics Tracking**: Monitor world population and events

## Game Modes

### Character Mode
Traditional RPG gameplay where you control a single character:
- Create and customize your character
- Explore the world and complete quests
- Engage in combat and level up
- Manage inventory and equipment
- Interact with NPCs and factions

### Simulation Mode
Watch an autonomous world evolve:
- Observe NPCs forming factions and relationships
- Witness wars, alliances, and political intrigue
- Track world statistics and population changes
- Discover emergent stories and events
- Analyze faction dynamics and power struggles

## Installation

1. Ensure you have Python 3.7+ installed
2. Clone or download this repository
3. Run `python main.py` to start the game

## Controls

- **Numeric Input**: Select menu options by entering numbers
- **Text Input**: Enter names, confirmations, and custom text
- **Navigation**: Use menu options to navigate between different game areas

## Game Systems

### Character Classes
- **Warrior**: High health and physical combat abilities
- **Mage**: Powerful magic but low physical stats
- **Rogue**: High agility and stealth abilities
- **Cleric**: Balanced stats with healing abilities
- **Ranger**: Skilled with ranged weapons and nature magic

### Races
- **Human**: Balanced stats with adaptability
- **Elf**: High intelligence and magical affinity
- **Dwarf**: High constitution and crafting skills
- **Orc**: High strength and physical combat
- **Halfling**: High agility and luck

### Stats
- **Strength**: Physical power and melee damage
- **Intelligence**: Magical power and spellcasting
- **Wisdom**: Mental fortitude and perception
- **Constitution**: Health and endurance
- **Agility**: Speed and evasion
- **Perception**: Awareness and ranged combat
- **Charisma**: Social influence and leadership

## Advanced Features

### Magic Schools
1. **Evocation**: Damage-dealing spells (Fireball, Lightning Bolt)
2. **Abjuration**: Protection and defensive spells (Shield, Magic Armor)
3. **Conjuration**: Summoning and teleportation (Summon Familiar, Teleport)
4. **Divination**: Information and detection spells (Detect Magic, Clairvoyance)
5. **Enchantment**: Mind-affecting spells (Charm Person, Sleep)
6. **Illusion**: Deception and trickery spells
7. **Necromancy**: Death and undead magic
8. **Transmutation**: Transformation and alteration spells

### Religious System
- **Good Deities**: Aethon (Light), Valeria (War), Sylvan (Nature)
- **Neutral Deities**: Thorne (Knowledge), Raven (Death), Forge (Crafting)
- **Evil Deities**: Malice (Destruction), Shadow (Deception), Plague (Disease)

### Factions
- **Good Factions**: Guardians of Light, Merchant Guild, Scholars Guild
- **Neutral Factions**: Adventurers Guild, Thieves Guild, Mages Circle
- **Evil Factions**: Cult of Darkness, Bandit Brotherhood, Necromancer Order

### Crafting Disciplines
- **Blacksmithing**: Weapons and armor
- **Alchemy**: Potions and magical items
- **Cooking**: Food and consumables
- **Enchanting**: Magical enhancements
- **Jewelcrafting**: Rings, amulets, and accessories

### Housing Features
- **Room Types**: Bedroom, Kitchen, Workshop, Library, Garden
- **Furniture**: Beds, tables, chairs, decorations, storage
- **Upgrades**: Improve room quality and functionality
- **Comfort**: Rest bonuses and stat improvements

### Companion Types
- **Dogs**: Loyal and protective companions
- **Cats**: Stealthy and independent allies
- **Horses**: Fast travel and combat mounts
- **Exotic**: Unique creatures with special abilities

## Tips for New Players

1. **Start with Character Mode**: Get familiar with the basic systems first
2. **Experiment with Different Classes**: Each class offers unique gameplay
3. **Manage Your Resources**: Gold, health, and mana are all important
4. **Build Relationships**: Reputation with factions opens new opportunities
5. **Explore Thoroughly**: You never know what you might find
6. **Save Regularly**: Don't lose your progress
7. **Try Simulation Mode**: Watch the world evolve on its own
